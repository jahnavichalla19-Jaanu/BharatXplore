❓ The Problem

Tourism in India is huge, but the experience is scattered. Travellers face:

🗣️ Language barriers with locals, vendors and guides
💸 Overcharging because prices are not fixed
👥 Overcrowded spots with no warning and no alternative
🏘️ Very little information about small towns and villages
👩 Safety concerns, especially for solo women travellers
💡 Our Idea

BharatXplore is one tourism platform that guides a traveller from the country level down to the village level, in their own language, with local information, fair prices, crowd awareness and safety tools in one place.

🧭 Planned User Journey
🌐 Choose your language
🗺️ See an interactive 3D map of India and click a state
🏛️ View the state's famous landmarks and personalities
🗂️ Browse districts as flashcards, then villages
📍 Open a village to see a local roadmap with restaurants, police stations, stays and more
🧳 Plan the trip with or without a package
✨ Planned Features
	Feature
🌐	Multi-language support
🌍	Built for both foreign and Indian tourists
🎙️	AI voice-to-voice translator
🔳	Vendor QR pass with fixed prices to prevent overcharging
🚦	Crowd alerts with automatic nearby alternate location suggestions
🎒	Optional bundled packages, trips without a package also supported
🔊	Location voice guide describing each spot's specialties and dangers
🃏	Flashcard-style information for districts and villages
🆘	SOS button that calls the nearest police station
🛡️	Solo-women safety flag per location, with a periodic check-in that alerts a saved emergency contact if unanswered
🧮	AI budget calculator for tourists
